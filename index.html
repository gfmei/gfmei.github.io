Research Focus

3D Vision & Language

Connecting 3D perception with language through multimodal models and semantic grounding.

Point Cloud Understanding

Learning geometric representations for point cloud registration, matching, and scene understanding.

World Models

Building generative models of 3D scenes and their underlying geometry.

Embodied AI

Connecting perception to robotic reasoning, grasping, and interaction.

Efficient Learning

Developing encoder-free architectures, compact representations, and self-supervised learning methods.
